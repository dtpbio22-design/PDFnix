    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: October 2026

    Your files

    PDFnix processes files inside your browser. Your files are not uploaded to our servers and are not stored or shared by us. When you close or reload the page, the files are gone from the tool.

    Accounts and personal data

    PDFnix does not require an account and does not ask for your name or email to use the tools.

    Third-party services

    The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and the PDF libraries from cdnjs. The PDF OCR tool also downloads its engine and language data from public servers the first time you use it. Your files are never sent to those servers. Those services may receive your IP address when they deliver files to your browser, as with any website.

    Advertising and analytics

    If we add advertising or analytics in future, this page will be updated to explain what is collected and how to opt out.

    Questions about privacy? Use the contact page.

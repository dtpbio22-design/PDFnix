    About PDFnix

    PDFnix is a free set of PDF tools that run inside your web browser. You can merge, split, rotate, number and watermark PDF files, and turn images into PDFs, without creating an account.

    Private by design

    Your files are processed on your own device. They are not uploaded to a server, so we never receive or store them.

    More tools are coming

    We add new tools step by step. If you need a tool that is not here yet, tell us on the contact page.
